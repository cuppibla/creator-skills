# Hook · 卡点 · Open-Loop Library (shared, bilingual)

Reach for this on almost any rewrite. These are fill-in-the-blank patterns, tuned for tech教学 but niche-agnostic. Keep Annie's 中英混用 register — many of these work mid-switch.

---

## Hook formulas (开头钩子)

The hook makes a **promise** (result / reveal / lesson / tension) and shows or says it instantly. Pick by the video's emotional angle.

**1. The contrarian / myth-break** — challenge a held belief
- "Everything you've been told about [agent memory] is wrong. Here's why."
- "你以为 [RAG] 解决了这个问题。其实没有。"

**2. The curiosity gap** — promise a specific, withheld payoff
- "There's one [ADK] setting that doubled my agent's accuracy. Almost nobody touches it."
- "[X] is the most important and the most overlooked part of building agents."

**3. The result-first / cold open** — show the finished thing, then rewind
- "This agent booked my flight while I slept. 10 minutes, let's build it."
- show the working demo running *before* any explanation

**4. The specific stat / proof** — lead with a number (best for tech/B2B)
- "I cut my agent's latency by 80% with three lines. Here they are."
- on-screen: the before/after metric, live

**5. The direct pain-point question** — name their exact frustration
- "Your agent forgets everything between turns, right? Fix it in 4 minutes."
- "为什么你的 multi-agent 系统总是 race condition?"

**6. The stakes/tension frame** — what breaks if they don't know this
- "#4 of these is the one quietly breaking your production app."

**Hook anti-patterns (kill on sight):** "Hi everyone, in today's video…" · "Before we start, don't forget to…" · a logo sting · a story about your day · stating the *topic* instead of the *promise* ("Today we'll talk about memory" → no tension, no reason to stay).

## Open-loop phrasebook (悬念 / 留存钩子)

Open a loop = plant a question the brain needs closed. Open early, close with a payoff, then open the next. Close each within 1–3 beats.

**Opening a loop:**
- "But there's a catch, and it's not the one you think…"
- "This works — until you hit [scale / production / the second agent]."
- "I'll show you the fix in a second, but first you need to see *why* it breaks."
- "等下你会看到一个 bug,而这个 bug 正是关键。"
- Signpost: "this next part is the bit everyone gets wrong."

**Closing a loop (the payoff):**
- "…and *that's* why it works."
- "There it is — [the result]. Now here's the problem that creates."
- The close should feel like a small click of satisfaction, then immediately hand to the next loop.

**Segment hand-off (prevents the mid-video drop):**
- "Solved. But that just moved the problem somewhere worse — segment 2."
- "搞定了第一个。第二个更难。"

**The final closed loop (don't skip):** end by explicitly closing the *main* loop from the hook, so the viewer feels their curiosity was paid off — then point forward (next video / next episode).

## 卡点 library (cut points / beat markers)

A 卡点 is any edit or beat where attention can snap — make each one earn the next stretch.

**Universal 卡点 (both formats):**
- **The flip** — before→after, wrong→right, expectation→reality. Strongest single beat. Place at the curiosity peak.
- **The wall** — the bug, the error, the "wait that didn't work." Don't smooth it out; it's the best teaching tension you have.
- **The reveal pause** — a half-beat of held silence/"here's the thing" right before a payoff. Earns the reveal.
- **The number/visual punch** — slam the metric or diagram on screen at the moment you say it.

**Long-form 卡点:**
- Section transition = title card + one-line open loop for what's next (never silent)
- Mid-roll engagement prompt, once, phrased to keep momentum
- Pre-climax pause before delivering the hook's promised answer

**Shorts 卡点:**
- Frame-1 pattern interrupt (hard cut / snap-zoom / whip-pan)
- A cut every ~1–2s, each advancing the idea
- The loop-back: last 0.5s ≈ frame 1
- No dissolves, no flat audio in the first 5s

## 节奏 quick-reference (pacing)

| | Long-form 8–15min | Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| Hook deadline | 15s | 2.5s |
| Baseline cut | every 15–25s | every 1–2s |
| Pattern | calm → burst (every 2–3 min) → calm | relentless, cut-to-idea |
| Worst sin | the mid-video sag | the 3-second cliff |
| Loop horizon | close within 1–3 beats / segment | whole thing is one loop |
| Ending | cliffhanger → next video | loop to frame 1 + next step |

## Diagnosing a retention graph (when Annie shares analytics)

- **Cliff at 0–2s (Short) / 0–15s (long):** weak promise. Fix line one.
- **Steady early bleed then plateau:** answer buried too early, or hook over-promised. Re-tease.
- **Mid-video cliff:** dead air — a stretch with no open loop, no 卡点, no visual change. Cut the tangent or insert a loop.
- **End spike / >100% segments:** a loop or rewatch-worthy moment — note what it was and do more of it.
- **Spike-and-drop after intro:** intro energy didn't bridge into segment 1. Smooth the hand-off, don't let the floor fall out.

Treat each upload's graph as the real benchmark. The numbers in SKILL.md are starting targets; her own curve, upload to upload, is the ground truth.
